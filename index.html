IV = 90-day constant-maturity ATM implied vol (total-variance interpolation between listed expiries). IV pctile ranks today against this name's own stored history (shown once ≥ days exist; until then the count is shown). RV pctile ranks 20-day realized vol against 2 years of prices, a stand-in while IV history builds. Quality: A = market-traded / quoted ATM options; B = model or exchange-settlement vol, or Yahoo IV without live quotes; C = unchanged for several days (stale); RV = no listed options, realized vol only. E = earnings within the 90-day tenor. Indicative only; get a tradeable quote.
